# Scene: The Drawn Blades

A sharp whistle cuts through the noise of the bazaar. Your movements have been leaked. Palace tax collectors and heavy city marshals form a tight ring around you, their iron-tipped spears leveled directly at your chest.

The market crowd grows dead quiet. Merchants scramble to protect their clay jars of oil and spice baskets. "Yield the contraband and step forward for shackling!" the platoon leader commands.

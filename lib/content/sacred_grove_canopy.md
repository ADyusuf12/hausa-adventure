# Scene: The Whispering Leaves

Deep within the Tamarind grove, ancient wooden ladders scale the trunks into an elevated platform woven from raw bark ropes. The modern court’s authority cannot reach up here; the air feels heavy with old, unfiltered ancestral memories.

The ritual elders offer you a Choice: entrench your soul into the land's defensive identity, or take a weapon of pure intent back down to the mortal courts.

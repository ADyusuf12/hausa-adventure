# Scene: Retreat from the Abyss

Your blade strikes wide, ringing loudly against the ancient well perimeter. Before you can recover your stance, Sarki rises, eyes flashing with a cold, ancient fury. The sacred charm flares with a protective light, absorbing a burst of dark spiritual energy that would have been fatal—but the sheer force throws you backward onto the dry earth.

Weakened and empty-handed, you are forced to retreat to the edge of the market while the serpent hisses its dominance.
